Biography
  Starting his career in facade design and construction documentation, actively involved in shop drawing production, detail development, and coordination. Following this, architectural design, 3D coordination, and construction documentation processes were led for large-scale mixed-use, defense, and energy infrastructure projects. Active responsibility was assumed in interdisciplinary applications for steel and concrete integration in industrial buildings. Utilizing advanced digital tools in alignment with a BIM-oriented design approach, high technical accuracy and sustainable solutions are maintained as the core professional practice.
