BIM Case Study: Large-Scale Mixed-Use Development
Typology: Residential · Mixed-Use
Location: Tashkent, Uzbekistan
Scale: 150,000 m²
Project Phase: Construction Documentation
BIM Level: LOD 350
Status: Under Construction
Role: Architectural BIM Lead
Engagement: Independent Studio — Cosmos.Lab
Key Deliverables: Construction documentation for vertical circulation systems · Stairs, ramps, escalators and elevator shafts · Full architectural drawing sets for execution phase

THE CHALLENGE

Vertical circulation systems — staircases, ramps, escalators and elevators — are the most geometrically complex and coordination-intensive elements in any large-scale mixed-use development. They run the full height of the building, intersect with structural cores at every level, and carry MEP shafts in their immediate proximity. A single unresolved conflict in a staircore can cascade into structural, mechanical and fire safety issues simultaneously.

On a 150,000 m² development, delivering these systems at construction documentation level means producing drawing sets precise enough for contractors to build from directly — with no room for interpretation on site.

METHODOLOGY

- Construction Documentation: The complete architectural drawing sets were produced for all vertical circulation systems across the full building height — staircases, ramps, escalators and elevator shafts. Each system required resolving the intersection between structural geometry, architectural envelope and MEP shaft routing at every floor level.

- Geometric Coordination: The staircore and elevator shaft zones were modelled and documented in full detail — tread and riser geometry, landing configurations, shaft wall assemblies, penetration positions and clearance zones. Every element was cross-referenced against the structural and MEP models before sheet issue.

- Drawing Set Production: Plans, sections, elevations and assembly details were produced to execution standard — dimensioned, annotated and coordinated for direct contractor use during the construction phase.

RESULTS & VALUE ADDED

Vertical circulation is the element contractors build first and build fast. Delivering a precise, fully coordinated documentation set for these systems meant the construction teams had an unambiguous reference from day one — removing the most common source of early-phase site queries and structural coordination problems before they could arise.
